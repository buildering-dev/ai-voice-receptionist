The calls your agents can't pick up

When an agent is with a client, at an open home or in an appraisal, this AI assistant answers their phone: it answers questions about listings, books inspections and takes the details so the agent can call straight back. Click the button, allow your microphone, and talk to it like a real caller.

Things to try:
  • "Hi, I'm calling about the apartment in Lavender Bay. What's the price guide?"
  • "When can I inspect the Willoughby house? Can you book me in for Saturday?"
  • "I'd like a free appraisal of my place."
  • "I want to put an offer in on the Willoughby house."

The agency, listings and agents are fictional. Calls are capped at a few minutes.
